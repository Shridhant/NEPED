## Project Summary
Sensitizing artisans and NGO personnel on the importance of brand building for unique ethnic handicraft items.

## Project Historical Overview & Operational Methodology
Addressing the gap between exceptional hand craftsmanship and modern commercial packaging, NEPED conducted intensive brand building seminars.

Artisans were trained in product standardization, trademark registration, storytelling, eco-friendly packaging, pricing psychology, and direct-to-consumer digital marketing via social media.

## Key Strategic Mandates & Objectives
1. Train rural craftspersons in brand identity, trademarking, and product narrative crafting.
2. Introduce sustainable paper and woven bamboo packaging for luxury ethnic exports.
3. Equip artisans with digital cataloging, photography, and mobile payment skills.
4. Build managerial capacity among craft cooperative leaders and local SHG federations.

## Major Milestones & Institutional Deliverables
* Conducted 8 comprehensive masterclasses across district craft headquarters.
* Over 350 rural artisans and NGO facilitators certified in creative brand entrepreneurship.
* Helped 24 artisan collectives register custom brand logos and social commerce storefronts.
* Distributed standardized product packaging kits crafted from recycled mountain fibres.
