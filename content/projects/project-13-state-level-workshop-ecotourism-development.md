## Project Summary
Empowering village communities through eco-tourism: conservation, sustainable livelihoods, and cultural hospitality.

## Project Historical Overview & Operational Methodology
Funded by the North Eastern Council (NEC), this state-level strategic mission harnesses Nagaland's unmatched bio-cultural diversity to create high-value, low-impact community ecotourism circuits.

NEPED trains village youth and homestay hosts in nature guiding, safety protocols, waste management, cultural storytelling, and digital reservation systems, turning living forests into permanent community economic engines.

## Key Strategic Mandates & Objectives
1. Develop a sustainable community ecotourism roadmap for Nagaland's Community Conserved Areas.
2. Train village youth as certified trekking guides, birdwatching naturalists, and rescue responders.
3. Establish standards for eco-homestays utilizing traditional bamboo and timber architecture.
4. Create direct online discovery and booking channels for international and domestic nature travelers.

## Major Milestones & Institutional Deliverables
* Comprehensive state workshop convening Village Council chairpersons, tourism experts, and homestay hosts.
* Drafting of the standard 'Community Eco-Tourism Code of Conduct' for protected wildlife zones.
* Certification of 150+ village guides in wilderness first-aid, bird identification, and visitor hospitality.
* Pilot launching of 6 community-managed trekking and birding circuits across pristine mountain ridges.
